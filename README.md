## 📌 Problem Statement  
95% of Indians do not have a structured financial plan. Traditional financial advisors charge ₹25,000+ per year and mainly cater to high net-worth individuals, leaving the majority without accessible guidance.

---

## 💡 Solution  
AI Money Mentor is an AI-powered personal finance assistant that simplifies financial planning. It helps users manage savings, investments, taxes, and life goals through an intuitive, conversational interface—making financial planning as easy as chatting on WhatsApp.

---

## ✨ Key Features  

### 📊 FIRE Path Planner  
- Calculates financial independence goals  
- Provides month-by-month investment roadmap  
- Suggests SIPs, asset allocation, and savings targets  

### 💯 Money Health Score  
- 5-minute onboarding  
- Evaluates financial health across:
  - Emergency fund  
  - Insurance  
  - Investments  
  - Debt  
  - Tax efficiency  
  - Retirement readiness  

### 🎯 Life Event Advisor  
- Personalized advice for:
  - Salary bonus  
  - Marriage  
  - New baby  
  - Major expenses  

### 🧾 Tax Wizard  
- Identifies missed deductions  
- Compares old vs new tax regime  
- Suggests tax-saving investments  

### 👩‍❤️‍👨 Couple Financial Planner  
- Joint financial planning  
- Optimizes tax benefits (HRA, NPS)  
- Tracks combined net worth  

### 📈 Portfolio X-Ray  
- Analyzes mutual fund portfolio  
- Calculates XIRR  
- Detects overlaps and inefficiencies  
- Suggests rebalancing strategies  

---

## 🧠 How It Works  

1. User inputs financial data (income, expenses, goals)  
2. AI processes and analyzes financial profile  
3. Generates personalized financial plan  
4. Provides actionable insights:
   - Investment strategy  
   - Tax optimization  
   - Savings improvement  
5. Continuously adapts recommendations
